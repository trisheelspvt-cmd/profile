Background & Philosophy

I’m Trisheel S, a BBA Aviation Management student at Acharya Bangalore B-School (Bangalore University, 2024–27). I completed my 12th at Sri Ambal Thulasi Public School in 2024 and my 10th at Faith Academy in 2022, both under CBSE.
